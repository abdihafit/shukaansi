# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Your privacy is an amanah, a trust. Allah commands us to "render trusts to those to whom they are due" (Quran 4:58). This policy explains in plain words what we collect, why we collect it, who can see it and the choices you have.

## In short

- We never sell your data.
- We do not show adverts, and we do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
- The app contains no advertising or analytics trackers.
- Your email, phone number, date of birth and verification selfie are never shown to other members.
- You can delete your account and your data at any time from My profile.

## 1. Who we are

SHUKAANSI ("we", "us") runs the SHUKAANSI app and is responsible for your personal data. You can contact us at admin@shukaansi.com.

## 2. What we collect

Information you give us:

- Account: your email address and password. Your password is handled by our sign-in provider, Firebase Authentication. We never see it.
- Profile: name, gender, nationality, ethnicity, country and city of residence, profession, education, religious practice, marital status, marriage intentions and your introduction.
- Private details: date of birth and phone number.
- Photos: up to six profile photos, and whether you choose to blur them.
- Verification: the selfie you take for verification, and the reviewer's decision.
- Safety: reports you make, members you block, and photo requests you send or approve.
- Interest and matches: the members you show interest in, the members who show interest in you, and your matches.
- Partner preferences: the ages, countries, religious practice and marital status you are looking for. Only you can see them; we use them to choose your "For you" feed.
- Membership: your plan (Free, Premium or VIP), when it ends, and which of our administrators added it.
- Messages: the messages you send to and receive from your matches, and when you last read each conversation.
- Wali: if you add a Wali (guardian), their name, the invite code you shared, and their approval or refusal of each match. If you join as a Wali, your name and the members you look after.
- Settings: your language choice and which notifications you have seen, stored only on your device.

Information created automatically:

- Our provider, Google Firebase, processes technical information needed to sign you in and protect the service, such as your IP address, device information and sign-in times.

We do not collect your GPS location, your contacts, advertising identifiers or analytics data.

Sensitive information: your religious practice and ethnicity are sensitive data. We use them only because you choose to include them in your profile, with your explicit consent, to help you find a compatible spouse. You can change them, or delete your account, at any time.

## 3. Why we use it

- to create and run your account;
- to show your profile to suitable members and show you theirs;
- to check that you are 18 or older, using your date of birth;
- to verify photos and keep the community safe, including reviewing reports and preventing abuse;
- to contact you about your account, for example email verification and password reset;
- to meet our legal obligations.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

Where UK or EU data protection law applies, we rely on: our agreement with you (to provide the service), your explicit consent (for sensitive information), our legitimate interest in keeping members safe, and our legal obligations.

## 4. Who can see your information

Other members: only signed-in members of the opposite gender who are themselves listed can see your profile, and only after you are listed (your email is verified and you have at least one photo). They see your public profile: your name as you entered it, your age (not your date of birth), city and country, background, profession, education, religious practice, marital status, marriage intentions, introduction, photos (blurred if you choose, until you approve them) and your verified badge.

Interest and matches: when you show interest in someone, they can see that you did. When you both show interest, you match, and you can both see each other's photos, even if you chose to blur yours. Unmatching or blocking ends this.

Wali: your Wali sees your name and, for each match, the other member's public details without photos, so they can approve or decline. If you match with someone who has a Wali, their Wali sees your public details without photos. Both members see whether a Wali has approved.

VIP: if you have VIP, your profile is shown first in Discover, so other members may notice.

Messages: only you and the member you are talking to can read your conversation in the app. Your Wali does not see your messages. Our administrators can access stored messages only to investigate a safety report or where the law requires.

Never shown to other members: your email, phone number, date of birth, verification selfie, partner preferences, reports and blocks.

Our team: authorised staff see only what their role needs. Reviewers see your verification selfie and main photo. Moderators see reports. Administrators can see account details, including private details, to handle safety and support issues.

Service providers: we use Google Firebase to store data and run the service. Google processes data only on our instructions. Your data is stored in Google data centres in Europe. When paid memberships launch, payments will be handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store, and we will not see your full card details.

Legal reasons: we may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect someone from serious harm.

We do not share your data with anyone else.

## 5. How long we keep it

- We keep your data while your account is open.
- When you delete your account, we permanently delete your profile, private details, partner preferences, photos, verification selfie, Discover listing, photo access records, interests, matches, conversations, Wali links, invites and match requests, and your sign-in account.
- When a match ends, by unmatching or blocking, the conversation and its messages are deleted.
- Reports and block records may be kept after deletion where needed to protect other members and prevent abuse.
- Our provider may take a short time to remove deleted data from its systems.

## 6. Security

- Firebase encrypts data in transit and at rest.
- Strict access rules apply: your private details can be read only by you and our administrators.
- Your photos are never available through a public link.

No system is perfectly secure. Please use a strong password and keep it to yourself.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- get a copy of your data;
- correct it (you can edit your profile in the app);
- delete it (you can delete your account in the app);
- object to or restrict how we use it;
- withdraw your consent;
- move your data to another service;
- complain to a data protection authority, such as the ICO in the UK or the authority in your country.

To use these rights, email admin@shukaansi.com.

## 8. Children

SHUKAANSI is only for adults aged 18 or over. If we learn that a member is under 18, we will close the account.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We will tell you in the app about important changes and ask you to accept them.

## 10. Contact us

Email admin@shukaansi.com with any question about your privacy.
