# Terms of Use

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Bismillah ir-Rahman ir-Rahim. In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful.

SHUKAANSI is a matrimonial service for Muslims who are seeking marriage (nikah). These terms set out the rules for using the app. They are written to reflect Islamic manners (adab) as well as the law. By creating an account you agree to them. If you do not agree, please do not use SHUKAANSI.

## 1. Our purpose

SHUKAANSI exists to help Muslims find a spouse in a halal and dignified way. Allah says: "And among His signs is that He created for you spouses from among yourselves so that you may find tranquillity in them, and He placed between you affection and mercy." (Quran 30:21)

SHUKAANSI is not a place for relationships outside marriage. Every introduction and conversation made through SHUKAANSI should have marriage as its intention.

## 2. Who may use SHUKAANSI

You may use SHUKAANSI only if:

- you are at least 18 years old;
- you are a Muslim;
- you are seeking marriage and are free to marry under Islamic law and the law of the country where you live;
- you have not previously been removed from SHUKAANSI; and
- you have not been convicted of a violent or sexual offence.

## 3. Honesty (amanah)

Islam commands truthfulness, and deception in marriage causes great harm. You agree that:

- everything in your profile is true and about you, including your name, age, marital status, location and religious practice;
- your photos show you, are recent, and are not edited to mislead;
- you will state your marital status honestly;
- you will hold only one account and use it only yourself.

## 4. Islamic conduct (adab)

When using SHUKAANSI you agree to:

- speak with respect, kindness and modesty, guarding your words as you guard your gaze (Quran 24:30-31);
- keep conversations purposeful and focused on marriage;
- involve your family or wali where appropriate. We encourage every member, and especially our sisters, to involve a wali or a trusted family member;
- avoid backbiting, spying, mockery and bad suspicion of others (Quran 49:11-12);
- respect a person's decision if they are not interested, and not contact them again.

Sisters must add a Wali before they can message a match, and anyone who adds a Wali needs their approval first. A Wali must be a genuine guardian or trusted family member, use their own account, and use it only to approve or decline matches.

You must not:

- send or ask for sexual, flirtatious or indecent messages or images;
- harass, threaten, insult or pressure anyone;
- ask anyone for money, gifts, bank details or financial help, or offer them;
- advertise or sell anything, or recruit anyone for any organisation;
- ask to meet anyone alone in private (khalwa);
- use SHUKAANSI for anything unlawful or contrary to Islamic teachings.

## 5. Photos and modesty

- Photos must be of you and must be modest. Men and women should be dressed modestly, in line with Islamic guidance.
- Photos of children or other people, nudity, sexual poses and offensive content are not allowed.
- Sisters may choose to blur their photos so that only members they approve can see them.
- The verification selfie is used only for verification and is never shown on your profile. Sisters who wear the niqab are asked to show their face for this private check, which only authorised reviewers can see.

## 6. Verification

We may ask you to complete a selfie verification. A reviewer compares your selfie with your main photo. The verified badge means only that the photos appear to show the same person. It does not confirm your identity documents, background, marital status, character or intentions. Changing your main photo removes the badge until you verify again.

## 7. Your safety

SHUKAANSI does not carry out criminal background checks. Please take care:

- never send money to someone you have met on SHUKAANSI;
- keep your address, workplace and financial details private until you trust the person;
- meet for the first time in a public place, with your wali or family present;
- report anyone who behaves inappropriately. You can block or report any member from their profile.

## 8. Reports and moderation

Our team reviews reports. We may remove content, hide your profile from Discover, or suspend or permanently close your account if we believe you have broken these terms or put others at risk. Where we can, we will tell you why. If you believe we have made a mistake, please contact us.

## 9. Memberships

Free members can create a profile, get verified, browse Discover and show interest. A match needs Premium or VIP on at least one side: when two members show interest in each other and either has Premium or VIP, the match is made.

- Premium costs £4.99 per month: match with anyone who also shows interest in you, and message your matches (with Wali approval where required).
- VIP costs £14.99 per month: everything in Premium, and your profile is shown first in Discover.

Until subscriptions through Google Play are available, our team may add a plan to your account for a set period. When subscriptions are available, they are charged by Google Play or the App Store, renew automatically unless you cancel in your store account settings, and follow the store's refund rules. Prices may be shown in your local currency.

## 10. Your content

## 11. Closing your account

You can delete your account at any time from My profile. This permanently removes your profile, photos and private details, as described in our Privacy Policy.
